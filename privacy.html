Privacy policy

yt-analytics-personal runs locally on the user's own computer. It requests read-only access (yt-analytics.readonly, youtube.readonly) to the user's own YouTube channel data.

OAuth tokens and analytics data are stored only on the user's device. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's APIs. No data is sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
