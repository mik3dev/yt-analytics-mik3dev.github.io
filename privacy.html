Privacy policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

What the tool accesses

With the user's consent, yt-analytics-mik3dev requests read-only access to the user's own YouTube channel data, using these scopes:

  • yt-analytics.readonly: channel and video analytics
  • youtube.readonly: channel and video titles and publish dates

Where data is stored

The tool runs locally on the user's own computer. OAuth tokens and analytics data are stored only on that device. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's APIs.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with third parties, and none is used for advertising.

Revoking access

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
