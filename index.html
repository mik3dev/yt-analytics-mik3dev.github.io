yt-analytics-mik3dev

A private, read-only tool that lets YouTube channel owners view their own channel analytics through the YouTube Analytics API.

  • Runs locally on the owner's own computer.
  • Read-only: it cannot upload, edit, or delete anything.
  • Used only by the owners of the channels it connects to.
  • Not a public service; there is nothing to sign up for.
